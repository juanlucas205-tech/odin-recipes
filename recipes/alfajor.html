Alfajor

Alfajor is a traditional Argentine cookie made of two wafers filled with dulce de leche.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups of flour
  • 1/2 cup of butter
  • 1/2 cup of sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup of dulce de leche

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Mix the flour, butter, and sugar to create the dough.
  3. Roll out the dough and cut into circles.
  4. Bake for 10-12 minutes or until golden brown.
  5. Fill each cookie with dulce de leche and press together.
