Fideos con tuco

Fideos con tuco is a traditional Argentine pasta dish with a flavorful tomato-based sauce.

Ingredients:

  • 200g of pasta
  • 1 cup of tomato sauce
  • 1/2 cup of onions
  • 1/2 cup of bell peppers
  • 1 lb of ground beef
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Cook the pasta according to the package instructions.
  2. In a pan, sauté the onions and bell peppers until soft.
  3. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
  4. Stir in the tomato sauce and season with salt and pepper.
  5. Combine the cooked pasta with the sauce and serve hot.
