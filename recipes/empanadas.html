Empanadas

Argentinian empanadas are a delicious pastry filled with meat, vegetables, or cheese.

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup of flour
  • 1/2 cup of butter
  • 1/2 cup of milk
  • 1 lb of ground beef
  • 1/2 cup of onions
  • 1/2 cup of bell peppers
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Mix the flour, butter, and milk to create the dough.
  3. Roll out the dough and cut into circles.
  4. Fill each circle with the meat mixture.
  5. Fold the edges to seal and place on a baking sheet.
  6. Bake for 20-25 minutes or until golden brown.
